ONYX Drive HUD v5.2.7d Vehicle HUD No-AI Notes

This build removes the experimental AI Coach and local AI connector from the prototype branch.

Why:
- Odysseus currently requires extra setup such as Docker and/or model/API configuration.
- That is not suitable for a normal public ONYX release.
- ONYX should remain free, simple and immediately usable without API keys.

What remains:
- Vehicle ID / CarOrdinal parser work.
- Fixed vehicle badge in the overlay.
- Vehicle status labels across the manager UI.
- car_database.json mapping support.
- Performance Lab, Smart Hints, Grip Monitor, Drag Timer, Dyno and existing telemetry tools.

Test focus:
1. Confirm ONYX starts cleanly.
2. Confirm no AI Coach / Odysseus / Ollama controls are visible.
3. Confirm vehicle badge still appears in the overlay.
4. Confirm Performance Summary still works.
5. Confirm existing HUD tiles, presets, layout save and units still work.
